How it works

Three things happen after every review.

No dashboard to check, no template to fill in — Tend handles the reply, the judgment call, and the follow-up post.

Replies that sound like you

Tend learns from replies you've already written — not a generic script. It names the technician, mentions the actual service, and never opens the same way twice in a row.

A human stays in the loop

Anything with real risk — a health complaint, a legal threat, a scheduling failure — gets held and sent to your phone. Approve, edit, or reject in one tap.

Best reviews, made shareable

Every five-star review becomes a branded quote card, sized for each platform — your logo and colors kept in, the reviewer's identity always left out.
